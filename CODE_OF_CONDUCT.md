# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to making participation in Mage-OS Installer respectful, constructive, and welcoming to everyone.

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- using inclusive and considerate language;
- respecting different experience levels, backgrounds, and viewpoints;
- accepting constructive feedback;
- focusing on what is best for the project and its users;
- showing empathy toward other contributors.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, sexualized language or attention, publishing private information without permission, and any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for serious or repeated violations.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainers through the repository's available private contact channel. Include what happened, when it happened, the relevant links or context, and any immediate safety concerns. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces and to interactions representing the project.
